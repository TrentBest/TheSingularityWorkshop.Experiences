# Open questions and decisions

This file protects the distinction between captured vision and settled design. Resolve questions deliberately; do not invent answers merely to make the document look complete.

## Identity and universe
- What is the Experience's final name? "Galactic War" is only a neutral working folder/title.
- Is this a wholly original universe, a raWWar successor/spiritual relative, or a distinct Experience that shares selected design DNA?
- What is the central conflict, and why is the enemy expanding or attacking?
- What makes the enemy's overwhelming numbers possible, and what are its discoverable weaknesses?
- What does "technological magic" mean in the world's own vocabulary?

## Player and learning
- Who is the intended audience and age range?
- Is the player explicitly a child/learner in the fiction, or is early learning a broader civilization-level premise?
- What does training look like in-world?
- How are knowledge and competence assessed without turning play into a test?
- Which roles are available, and can players change roles naturally?

## Mission design
- How does the player receive enough clues to survive and discover the real mission?
- How much uncertainty is exciting, and when does it become confusion?
- Can missions be failed, abandoned, recovered, or solved in unexpected ways?
- How do preparation and logistics remain engaging rather than becoming chores?

## Combat and vehicles
- What is the first-person combat loop before spacecraft are introduced?
- What are the first exoskeleton archetypes?
- What is the first dogfight craft and its control model?
- How do target lock, independent rotation, drift, and axis thrusters work together?
- What does the gravity field compensate for, and what are its limits?
- Which ship-size transitions introduce new roles and mechanics?

## Galaxy and social model
- Is the galaxy shared, shard-based, instance-based, or a hybrid?
- Which state is persistent and authoritative?
- How do alliances, factions, crews, and diplomacy work?
- How are griefing, harassment, exploitation, and unsafe contact prevented—especially if children are in the audience?
- What can be accomplished solo, cooperatively, or asynchronously?

## First vertical slice
- What is the smallest slice that demonstrates the signature promise?
- Should the first slice emphasize survival-to-mission-discovery, exoskeleton combat, or the spacecraft dogfight fantasy?
- Which existing Workshop domain capabilities can be reused immediately?
- Which capabilities must be implemented elsewhere before this Experience can consume them?

## Production constraints
- Which target manifestations are first: desktop, browser, or VR?
- What are the performance and accessibility requirements?
- What is the content-authoring pipeline for characters, missions, ships, and factions?
- What is the minimum safe online interaction model?

## Current decisions
- The project is captured under experiences/GalacticWar/ as its own Experience preforge.
- The folder/title "Galactic War" is provisional, not the final brand.
- raWWar is a combat-style reference; this concept is not assumed to replace or mutate raWWar.
- This commit is design/documentation only. No gameplay implementation, manifest, package identity, or publication is being claimed.
- Future implementation must reuse existing Experience, MicroBundle, FSM_COS, host, and domain contracts rather than recreate infrastructure.
