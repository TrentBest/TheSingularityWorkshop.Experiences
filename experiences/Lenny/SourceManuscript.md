## Story

### Page 1

Little Lenny sat in the warm sand on the beach, digging
in the sand and filling the holes with ocean water. As he
ran back to the ocean waves to fill his bucket, he saw his
dad picking up something from the ground.

### Page 2

He ran as fast as he could to see what his dad was
finding! His bucket sloshed around, spilling water along
the beach as he ran.
“Dad! What are you finding?”, Lenny asked excitedly, out
of breath.

### Page 3

Lenny’s dad looks up and laughs, “I am picking up this
trash I found, Lenny! Would you like to help me?”

Lenny stops and stares up at his dad and then glances
down at the plastic water bottle in his dad’s hands.

“That isn’t our trash! Why are you
picking it up?”, Lenny asks, curiously.

### Page 4

“You are right, Lenny, this is not our trash. But I want
to pick it up so that the beach will be clean.”, his dad
replied.

Lenny looked around the beach, confused. “Why can’t
we just leave it there? It doesn’t seem to be bothering
anyone!”

### Page 5

“Well, we could just leave it there, but there is a lot
of sea life that could be harmed if we all just left our
trash on the beach.”, Lenny listened to his dad intently.
“Eventually all of this beach trash will end up in the
oceans. There is a lot of life in the ocean that we do not
see, and they do not want to swim in our trash.”

“It can make them very sick. It also hurts our water and
air.”, Lenny’s dad continued.

### Page 6

Lenny looked out to the ocean and sighed, “I want to help
them. I will help pick up the trash!”

“That is great, Lenny! I am so proud of you!”, his dad
replied.
Lenny had already run off to pick up a bottle he saw a few
feet away.
Lenny ran around the beach, happily picking up trash
and running it back to put it in the trash bag his dad was
holding.

### Page 7

After only a few minutes they had already cleaned up all
the trash they could see in that part of the beach. Lenny
looked around, proud of their work.

### Page 8

The next day, Lenny and his family returned to the
beach. He ran quickly out to the sand, expecting to see
the clean beach they had worked so hard on the day
before.

He stopped suddenly and looked around. There were a
lot of people enjoying the beach, and there was already
so much trash laying around again.

### Page 9

Lenny suddenly became very sad. He turned around and
said, “Dad! It is filled with trash again! What do we do?”.
He felt very discouraged and sat down, dropping his
bucket and shovel next to him.

“It’s ok, Lenny! Don’t get discouraged!”, his dad said,
kneeling beside him. “Why don’t we play a little bit and
then we can clean up before we leave, just like we did
yesterday?”.

### Page 10

Lenny lifted his head and looked up at his dad, “I am
just so worried about the sea animals, Dad. We can’t pick
up all the trash, there is so much!”. He kicked the sand
around with his feet, staring at the ground.

“Lenny, I understand why you are frustrated. It feels like a
very big problem, but if we all help just a little bit, doing
what we can, that makes the problem easier to deal with.”,
he said, placing his arm around Lenny.

“It’s important in life to leave everything and everyone
better than you found them.”

### Page 11

Lenny leaned into his Dad for a moment, looking around
the beach. Suddenly, he jumped up and proclaimed
proudly, “That’s it! We just have to get everyone to help!
Come on, Dad, let’s go tell them about the sea animals! I
know they will help if they hear the danger the animals
are in!”.

### Page 12

Lenny ran off, picking up pieces of trash as he went. His
dad ran behind him, trying to keep up.

Lenny would run from piece of trash to piece of trash,
quickly spotting the next one. He wasn’t watching where
he was going as he ran across a sunbather’s towel, or
through a group of kids building a sandcastle.

His dad ran behind him, apologizing to the beach-goers
for his son’s sandy path, left in his wake. “Lenny! Slow
down! We don’t have to pick up all the trash at once!”

### Page 13

Lenny stopped and turned around, looking at his dad
confused. “Yes we do, Dad! We have to also tell all these
people about the sea life! They are in danger!”.

Lenny ran off again, stopping at each group on the
beach, showing them the dirty water bottle he had found,
explaining how it could harm a sea turtle or sea lion.
Most of the groups politely nodded along, smiling at the
small boy, so full of passion.

### Page 14

But some beach-goers were not as interested in Lenny’s
desperate message. One woman, lying on her back,
sunning herself on a bright beach towel turns and
shushes Lenny.

### Page 15

Lenny stood staring at the crowd, realizing no one had
gotten up to pick up any of the trash on the beach.
Even though he told them about the animals and water
and the pollution. No one was stopping their surfing or
sandcastle building or picnicking.

He felt hopeless standing there, seeing there was so
much more trash still to clean, and realizing there would
be even more tomorrow. And no one seemed to care!!

### Page 16

Lenny dropped the water bottle on the sand and sat
down. He stared at the sand, feeling defeated.

### Page 17

Lenny’s dad walked up to him and knelt down beside
him. “It’s ok, buddy. You can’t solve the whole big ocean
problem on your own! No one can do it alone.”

### Page 18

“But, they don’t seem to care, Dad. They didn’t want to
help.”, Lenny replied, still staring at the sand.

Lenny’s dad put his hand on his son’s shoulder and
tried to think of what he could say to help Lenny feel
better.

### Page 19

Suddenly, an aluminum can popped up from behind
them and dropped on the ground next to them. Lenny
reached over and picked it up and put it in his trash bag.
He kept his head down, still feeling overwhelmed by the
big ocean problem.

### Page 20

Another can popped up behind them and dropped
between them. Lenny went to grab that one when
several water bottles popped up and dropped around
them.

Lenny and his dad turn around to see where all this
trash is suddenly coming from. They turned around to
the ocean behind them, and thats when they saw it!

### Page 21

There was a dolphin! His head was bobbing up out of
the ocean waves. He seemed to be smiling at Lenny.

Lenny and his dad looked at each other and then back at
the dolphin, completely in awe.

The dolphin dove back in the water and then just as
quickly, popped back up with a piece of trash on his
head. The dolphin popped up higher and tossed the
trash right into Lenny’s trash bag.

“It looks like you found some help!”, Lenny’s dad
laughed, pointing at the dolphin.

### Page 22

They ran around picking them up as quickly as they
could, laughing as they went. As they got closer to the
ocean, the dolphins would get closer to the beach.

Lenny suddenly ran into the ocean with his bag to get
a bottle that was floating away with the waves, his dad
running behind.

### Page 23

Lenny’s dad caught up to him and grabbed him just in
time to miss the wave crashing in. He felt a strong pull in
the water at his feet. He put Lenny on his shoulders and
looked down.

One of the dolphins was looking back up at him, which
was quite surprising!

### Page 24

Lenny and his dad both pointed at the dolphin with
excitement, but just as quickly, the wave swirled around
them and they found themselves in a fast moving current,
being taken out to sea.

### Page 25

The pod of dolphins were creating a bubble of air for
them to breathe as they were taken on quite a journey.

The dolphins communicated with Lenny and his dad
inside their heads, making them feel calm and safe.

### Page 26

Lenny and his dad started to see why the dolphins had
taken them on this adventure. They went down deep to
the bottom of the ocean floor and saw piles of trash.

### Page 27

They went to coral reefs and saw dying coral and more
trash.

### Page 28

They were taken through a group of sea turtles, some
who have permanent damage from getting stuck in
pieces of trash in the ocean.

### Page 29

They saw schools of fish who are hungry and homeless
because trash has taken over their ecosystems.

There was so much trash in the ocean!! The sea life
was trying to live around it. They were trying so hard to
survive, despite the trash!

### Page 30

This made Lenny and his dad so sad! The dolphins
could sense the sadness of the boy and his father. Lenny
and his dad could sense the sadness of the dolphins and
other sea life.

### Page 31

As the dolphins returned them to the beach where they
met, Lenny asked the dolphin pod, “What can we do?
How can we help?”. He had tears in his eyes and looked
down at his small bag of trash. He realized how many
more bags were needed to hold all the trash he saw in
the ocean.

### Page 32

The dolphin looked into Lenny’s eyes, smiled, and
dropped back into the ocean.

Lenny looked up at his dad with panic. Lenny’s dad
laughed as the dolphin popped back up with a few
pieces of trash balanced on his nose. He tossed them
into the air and they all landed, one after another, in
Lenny’s trash bag. The dolphin smiled, waved his fin in
the air, and dove back down into the ocean.

### Page 33

Lenny laughed, picked up the trash bag, and walked off
with it over his shoulder. As he walked, he picked up the
trash he saw along the way. He was smiling because he
now understood that if everyone just helped a little bit,
doing what they can, things would get better.
