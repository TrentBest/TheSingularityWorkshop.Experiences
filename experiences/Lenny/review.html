Little Lenny: presentation review

A guided route through the current text-only presentation studies. Review the emotional movement in story order, then compare the page-level alternatives. These studies explore pacing and composition only; they do not change the manuscript or establish approved artwork.

Author approval boundary: all presentation approaches are provisional. The previous concept illustrations are excluded completely. No character appearance, dolphin species or markings, palette, visual style, or production artwork is proposed as canon here. The story reader and source manuscript remain unchanged.

Questions for the author

  • Does the emotional pacing feel true to the story and to Lenny?
  • Where should a reader naturally pause, and where should the story carry them forward?
  • Does the dolphin reveal feel earned after the preceding discouragement and interruption?
  • Does the underwater sequence give enough space to the discovery and shared empathy?
  • Which approaches feel promising, and which should be set aside?

For useful feedback, identify the page range, study, and named approach. Then say what feels right, what feels wrong or missing, and what you would change. A preferred approach is a direction to explore—not approval of final composition, character design, visual style, or artwork.
