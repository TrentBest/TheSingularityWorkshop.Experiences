# Story Forge has moved

Story Forge is owned by [StoryBuilder](https://github.com/TrentBest/StoryBuilder), not Experiences.

The implementation, tests, temporal-model overview, and living diagrams are being maintained in StoryBuilder:
- [Story Forge source project](https://github.com/TrentBest/StoryBuilder/tree/development/src/StoryBuilder.StoryForge)
- [Ownership and migration record](https://github.com/TrentBest/StoryBuilder/blob/development/docs/STORY_FORGE_OWNERSHIP_AND_MIGRATION.md)
- [Story Forge vision](https://github.com/TrentBest/StoryBuilder/blob/development/docs/STORY_FORGE_VISION.md)

Experiences remains responsible for reusable Experience definitions, MicroBundle lifecycle and CRUD, manifests, registration, discovery, and reusable artifacts. It must not depend on StoryBuilder.
